Our Journey of Precision

Click on the milestones to trace the evolution of our automotive precision over the last two decades.

workspace_premium Inception & Foundation (2004)

Founded as a single service bay in Gurgaon, Excel Autocare was established by senior automotive engineers with the vision of introducing cleanroom-standard diagnostics to Maruti Suzuki car owners.

verified_user Maruti Suzuki Official Authorization (2012)

Recognized for our perfect repair record, Suzuki officially certified Excel Autocare as an Authorized Service Partner. We expanded our workshop capacity to 10 diagnostic bays and introduced high-speed telemetry.

precision_manufacturing State-of-the-Art Body & Paint Shop (2018)

Launched our dust-free computerized oven painting booths and advanced frame alignment units, establishing ourselves as Gurgaon's premier body restoration center for luxury and passenger classes.

electric_car Digital Evolution & EV Support (2024)

Achieved our 50,000+ servicing milestone. Integrated real-time video diagnostic reporting via our online portal and introduced specialized servicing bays for Maruti's hybrid and upcoming electric drivetrains.
