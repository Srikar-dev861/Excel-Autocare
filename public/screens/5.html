Our Journey of Precision

Tap the milestones to trace our evolution.

workspace_premium Inception & Foundation (2004)

Founded as a single service bay in Gurgaon, Excel Autocare was established by senior automotive engineers with the vision of introducing cleanroom-standard diagnostics to Maruti Suzuki owners.

verified_user Suzuki Official Authorization (2012)

Officially certified as an Authorized Service Partner. We expanded our workshop capacity to 10 diagnostic bays and introduced high-speed digital telemetry.

precision_manufacturing Advanced Body & Paint Shop (2018)

Launched our dust-free computerized oven painting booths and advanced frame alignment units, establishing ourselves as Gurgaon's premier body restoration center.

electric_car Digital Evolution & EV Support (2024)

Achieved our 50,000+ servicing milestone. Integrated real-time video diagnostic reporting via our online portal and introduced specialized servicing bays for Maruti's hybrid and electric drivetrains.
